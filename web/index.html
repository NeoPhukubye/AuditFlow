AuditFlow

Two-agent support triage · policy compliance · automatic human escalation

How it works

  1. Drafter (Generator) classifies the ticket, diagnoses the cause and drafts a reply, grounded by a knowledge base.
  2. Auditor (Discriminator) strictly verifies the draft against company policy and either approves it or issues a fix command.
  3. Refine & Escalate the fix is fed back into the Drafter; if two attempts are rejected, the ticket is handed to a human.
